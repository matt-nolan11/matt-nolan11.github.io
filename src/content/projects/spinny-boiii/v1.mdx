## Build Process

The first version was built primarily from aluminum bar stock and sheet, machined on a basic benchtop mill. The learning curve was steep, but each mistake taught valuable lessons that informed later versions.

### Frame Construction

- **Material**: 6061 aluminum bar and sheet
- **Weight**: 2.8 lbs (too heavy for the weapon system)
- **Tools**: Benchtop mill, drill press, hand tools

### Weapon System

- **Type**: Vertical spinning disk
- **Material**: Mild steel (later upgraded to tool steel)
- **Motor**: Brushed motor with simple on/off control
- **Mount**: Direct rigid mounting (caused shock damage)

### Electronics

- **Drive**: Basic brushed motor controllers
- **Weapon**: Mechanical switch (very basic)
- **Radio**: Standard hobby RC system
- **Power**: 3S LiPo battery

## Competition Results

Version 1 competed in several local events but struggled with reliability issues. The rigid weapon mounting led to frequent damage, and the heavy frame limited agility.

**Notable Matches:**
- Often powerful enough to damage opponents
- Frequently disabled by shock damage to internal components
- Learned importance of proper weapon mounting and weight distribution

This version served its purpose as a learning platform and provided the foundation for significant improvements in later iterations.
