## Project Goals

The primary objectives for this robot are:

- **Competitive Performance**: Design a robot capable of winning matches in beetleweight competitions
- **Reliability**: Build robust systems that can survive multiple fights without major repairs  
- **Manufacturability**: Use accessible manufacturing methods (3D printing, basic CNC)
- **Learning Platform**: Develop skills in robotics, manufacturing, and combat robot design

## Combat Robot Fundamentals

Combat robots operate in a unique environment where durability and weapon effectiveness are paramount. Unlike other robotics applications, combat bots must be designed to both inflict and withstand significant damage while maintaining functionality.

**Key Design Constraints:**
- Weight limit of exactly 3.0 pounds including all components
- Safety requirements for electronics and weapon systems
- Arena compatibility (size, ground clearance, materials)
- Radio control requirements and fail-safe systems

## Manufacturing and Assembly

The robot uses a hybrid manufacturing approach combining different techniques for optimal results:

**CNC Machining:** The main frame is machined from 6061-T6 aluminum plate for maximum strength and precision. This provides the structural integrity needed to handle weapon reaction forces and impact loads.

**3D Printing:** Non-structural components like covers, mounts, and spacers are 3D printed in PETG for rapid iteration and weight optimization. This allows for quick design changes and custom-fit components.

**Commercial Components:** Motors, electronics, and hardware are sourced from specialized suppliers to ensure reliability and performance in the demanding combat environment.

### Assembly Process

1. **Frame Preparation**: Machine the main chassis and weapon mount from aluminum stock
2. **Electronics Integration**: Install motor controllers, radio receiver, and safety systems
3. **Drive System**: Mount drive motors and wheels with proper alignment
4. **Weapon Assembly**: Balance and mount the spinning disk with safety checks
5. **Testing and Tuning**: Comprehensive testing of all systems before competition
