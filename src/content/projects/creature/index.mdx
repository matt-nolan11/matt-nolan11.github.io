"Creature" is a hobbyweight (12-pound) combat robot designed for ruggedness and control. The design features a strong, compact lifter mechanism with a 3-stage gear reduction, a powerful 4-wheel drivetrain with the drive gearboxes integrated into the frame rails, and a variety of armor configurations to counter different weapon types. I started building this robot in 2023 with my teammate, Caleb Hecht (who deserves credit for the initial idea, as well as many of the design and manufacturing decisions), and we fine-tuned the design over the course of several iterations and competitions, culminating in a first-place finish at RCL Nationals 2025. No major design changes are planned at this time, but we'll continue to compete with Creature when we find the time. Read more about each version of the robot below.
